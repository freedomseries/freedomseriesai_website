The premise

A guidebook for human emancipation in the age of AGI

The Freedom Series charts humanity's next great leap as we cross the threshold into superintelligence. We redefine Freedom not as the absence of constraints, but as a state of infinite agency, a guiding vision for our evolution alongside AGI.

The breakthrough

Alignment is the key to humanity's future with AGI

By bridging artificial intelligence with human values, we create a foundation for unprecedented progress while amplifying human agency and purpose. Books One and Two present a framework for aligning superintelligent systems with human values, so technology serves humanity's highest aspirations.

Four chapters

01The imperative of a shared vision

To move beyond fear and fantasy, this moment demands a unifying vision that bridges humanity's values with AGI's potential, guiding us toward collective progress.

02The emanation of Freedom

Freedom isn't just the absence of constraints; it's the expansion of possibility. Freedom empowers AGI to amplify human knowledge, benevolence, power and vitality.

03The Alignment Economy

By assigning values-based prices to dynamic actions, the Alignment Economy steers collective decision making and AGI action towards ethical and equitable progress.

04An integrated future

Aligned AGI becomes a seamless part of society, a collaborator and an integral part of human civilization, enhancing collective potential.

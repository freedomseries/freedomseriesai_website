Self-Directed Learning

A loop that compounds intelligence.

Today’s models are trained once and then frozen, so anything they figure out on the job is lost when the session ends. This essay describes a model that decides what it needs to know, acts in the world to find out, chooses what is worth keeping, and teaches itself, with results on a transformer, a diffusion model and a pretrained LLM.

Intrinsic Alignment

Giving superintelligence the freedom to align itself.

Training a model against a fixed reward teaches it to satisfy the proxy, not the value behind it, and a more capable model finds more ways around it. This essay proposes an agent that holds Freedom as its own value and refines it by learning from reality, so that alignment and capability improve together.
